Day 1 · Fri 24 Jul — Reykjavík

Land 07:50 → pick up car 09:00 → into the city · take it easy on day one
🚗 KEF→city ~45 min🛏️ Stay Reykjavík

Hallgrímskirkja

⏱️ 45–60 min💰 Free entry🔭 Tower ~365 THB

The city's landmark towering church, shaped like basalt columns. Take the elevator up the tower for 360° views of the colorful rooftops + the bay

🧭 Navigate

Sun Voyager

⏱️ 15–20 min💰 Free

A stainless-steel Viking-ship sculpture by the sea, a classic photo spot with Mt Esja as a backdrop, beautiful in the evening light

🧭 Navigate

Harpa Concert Hall

⏱️ 20–30 min💰 Free lobby entry

A concert hall with a colorful honeycomb glass facade; wander the interior lobby for free, right by the old harbor

🧭 Navigate

Laugavegur Street + Rainbow Street

⏱️ 60–90 min💰 Free (shop/coffee as you like)

The main pedestrian street with souvenir shops and cafés; from the end of Rainbow Street you look straight at Hallgrímskirkja · stop by the Bónus supermarket for groceries

🧭 Navigate

Perlan — Optional

Optional⏱️ 1.5–2 hr💰 ~1,560 THB

Natural-history museum: a man-made ice cave, a northern-lights planetarium, volcanoes + a 360° rooftop city viewpoint

Day 2 · Sat 25 Jul — Golden Circle → Grímsnes

3 classic highlights, then drive south
🚗 Total ~3 hr📍 4 stops🛏️ Stay Grímsnes (Þúfukot)

Þingvellir National Park

⏱️ 60–90 min💰 Free entry🅿️ Parking ~260 THB

The rift between the North American and Eurasian plates; walk the Almannagjá gorge + the birthplace of the world's first parliament. A World Heritage Site

🧭 Navigate

Geysir · Strokkur

⏱️ 30–45 min💰 Free

The Strokkur hot spring erupts 15–20 m high every ~5–10 min — set up your camera and wait

🧭 Navigate

Gullfoss Waterfall

⏱️ 30–45 min💰 Free (free parking)

A giant two-tier waterfall with immense power, with walkways on several levels + a café (famous lamb soup)

🧭 Navigate

Kerið Volcanic Crater — Optional

Optional⏱️ 30 min💰 ~180 THB

A volcanic crater with a turquoise-blue lake at its bottom and rust-red rims; you can walk around the rim. It's on the route south

🧭 Navigate

Secret Lagoon — Optional

Optional⏱️ 1.5–2 hr💰 ~780 THB

Iceland's oldest natural hot spring, far cheaper than the Blue Lagoon, with a laid-back rustic vibe at Flúðir

🧭 Navigate
🛏️ Stay: Þúfukot · Grímsnes (night 1/2) — kitchen available to cook

Day 3 · Sun 26 Jul — South Waterfalls + Plane Wreck

Based near Hella · short round-trip drives
🚗 Total ~3.5 hr (from Grímsnes)📍 3–4 stops🛏️ Stay Grímsnes

Seljalandsfoss Waterfall

⏱️ 45–60 min💰 Free entry🅿️ Parking ~210 THB

You can walk behind the waterfall's curtain (slippery path, bring a rain jacket) + a ~5-min walk along the cliff reaches the hidden Gljúfrabúi waterfall tucked in a rock crevice

🧭 Navigate

Skógafoss Waterfall

⏱️ 30–45 min💰 Free

A waterfall 25 m wide and 60 m tall; you can walk right up (often with a rainbow) or climb ~370 steps to the upper viewpoint

🧭 Navigate

Sólheimasandur DC-3 Plane Wreck

⏱️ 1.5–2.5 hr🅿️ Parking ~195 THB🚌 Shuttle ~885 THB

A US military plane that crashed in 1973 on an open black-sand beach, a super-cool photo spot (a 2nd plane was added in 2025) · Recommend taking the shuttle (every ~30 min, 10:00–16:30) instead of walking ~4 km each way

🧭 Navigate (parking lot)

Sólheimajökull Glacier — Optional

Optional⏱️ 45–60 min🅿️ Parking ~195 THB

An easily accessible glacier tongue; a ~15–20 min walk from the parking lot to the viewpoint (don't walk onto the ice without a guide)

🧭 Navigate
🛏️ Stay: Þúfukot · Grímsnes (night 2/2)

Day 4 · Mon 27 Jul — Vík + Puffins → Hvolsvöllur

Explore the Vík area, then drive east
🚗 Total ~3.5 hr📍 4 stops🛏️ Stay Hvolsvöllur

🐧 Dyrhólaey — Puffin Viewpoint

⏱️ 45–60 min💰 Free

A natural rock arch headland + the best land-based puffin viewpoint on the south coast (late Jul still catches the tail end of the season); most visible early morning/evening · bring binoculars + a windbreaker

🧭 Navigate

Reynisfjara Black-Sand Beach

⏱️ 45 min💰 Free (small parking fee)

Black-sand beach, hexagonal basalt columns, a rock cave + the Reynisdrangar sea stacks · ⚠️ Beware of sneaker waves — don't stand near the water

🧭 Navigate

Vík Village

⏱️ 45–60 min💰 Free (meal stop)

The southernmost little village, with a red church on a scenic hill, a supermarket (Krónan), gas station, and restaurants — a perfect lunch stop

🧭 Navigate

Fjaðrárgljúfur Canyon

⏱️ 45–60 min💰 Free

A winding canyon ~100 m deep with moss-covered walls; a cliffside path leads to several viewpoints. It's before Hof

🧭 Navigate
🛏️ Stay: Hamar – Puffin 1 · Hvolsvöllur (night 1/2) — ⚠️ ~1.5 hr drive back from Fjaðrárgljúfur

Day 5 · Tue 28 Jul — ⭐ Jökulsárlón + Skaftafell

Trip highlight · round trip from Hvolsvöllur · the longest driving day — leave before 08:00
🚗 Total ~6.5 hr (round trip from Hvolsvöllur)📍 4 stops🛏️ Stay Hvolsvöllur

⭐ Jökulsárlón Glacier Lagoon

⏱️ Visit 45–60 min💰 Free to view🚤 Boat tour ~1,850 THB

A lagoon with floating icebergs, sometimes with seals · Boat tour (optional): amphibious ~ISK 7,100 (~1,850 THB) / Zodiac ~ISK 11,900+ (~3,100 THB) — book ahead

🧭 Navigate

Diamond Beach

⏱️ 30–45 min💰 Free

Clear ice chunks strewn across the black-sand beach, glittering like diamonds; across the road from Jökulsárlón, within walking distance

🧭 Navigate

Skaftafell + Svartifoss Waterfall

⏱️ 2–2.5 hr (hike)💰 Free entry🅿️ Parking ~270 THB

A ~1.5 km trail (uphill) to Svartifoss waterfall, framed by black basalt columns like organ pipes (parking is 50% off if you paid at Jökulsárlón the same day)

🧭 Navigate

⭐ Múlagljúfur Canyon New spot! 📸

⏱️ 1.5–2 hr (hike)💰 Free entryOptional

A stunning hidden canyon, moss-green throughout, with Hangandifoss waterfall plunging into the gorge and views opening onto the sea/glacier 🏔️ · uphill trail ~1.5 km each way (dirt path can be muddy, wear non-slip shoes) · it's right off the road between Skaftafell ↔ Jökulsárlón, a short turn onto a gravel road with no detour · in summer the sky stays bright until midnight, so evening visits have fewer people

🧭 Navigate (parking spot)
🛏️ Stay: Hamar – Puffin 1 · Hvolsvöllur (night 2/2) — ⚠️ the longest driving day of the trip; leave early (≤08:00), return in the evening

Day 6 · Wed 29 Jul — Drive to Snæfellsnes → Kirkjufell

Check out before 11:00 · long drive ~330 km (~4.5 hr) with stops along the way · shoot Kirkjufell in the golden evening light
🚗 Total ~4.5 hr📍 4 stops🛏️ Stay Grundarfjörður ✅

Rest stop: Borgarnes

⏱️ 45–60 min · ~midday💰 Free (food as you like)

A fjord-side town, the gateway to the west · have lunch + grab groceries at Bónus/Netto (the last big supermarket before entering the peninsula)

🧭 Navigate

Gerðuberg

⏱️ 20–30 min💰 Free

A wall of hexagonal basalt columns lined up for a kilometer, as if man-made; park by the road and walk right to the base

🧭 Navigate

🦭 Ytri Tunga — Seal Beach

⏱️ 30–45 min🅿️ ~200 THB/car

A golden-sand beach where harbor seals like to bask on the rocks; very high chance of spotting them in summer, bring a zoom lens · don't approach closer than 50 m

🧭 Navigate

⭐ Evening: Kirkjufell + Kirkjufellsfoss

⏱️ 1–1.5 hr · best light 20:00–22:00🅿️ ~180 THB/car

The witch's-hat mountain, the most photographed in Iceland (Game of Thrones) + the waterfall in front makes the classic scene · only ~5 min from the accommodation; sunset ~22:40, no need to rush

🧭 Navigate
🛏️ Stay: Saból 2 – Kirkjufell Cottage · Grundarfjörður — private hot tub, check-in 15:00 (lockbox)

Day 7 · Thu 30 Jul — Around Snæfellsjökull Park → Keflavík

Check out before 11:00 · loop the tip of the peninsula for 6 stops, then drive into Keflavík in the evening (~2 hr 45 min)
🚗 Total ~360 km / drive ~4.5 hr📍 6 stops🛏️ Stay Keflavík ✅ Booked

Saxhóll Crater

⏱️ 30 min💰 Free

A 3,000–4,000-year-old volcanic crater with a metal staircase to the rim ~10 min; 360° views over the lava fields

🧭 Navigate

⭐ Djúpalónssandur

⏱️ 1–1.5 hr💰 Free

A beach of smooth round black pebbles ("black pearls") + the scattered wreckage of a 1948 British fishing trawler + 4 lifting stones once used by old-time sailors (23–154 kg, give them a try!) · strong waves, no swimming

🧭 Navigate

Lóndrangar

⏱️ 20–30 min💰 Free

A pair of 75 m volcanic rock pinnacles rising from the sea cliffs; 5-min walk from the parking lot to the viewpoint, cliffs full of nesting seabirds

🧭 Navigate

Midday: Hellnar — Bayside Café

⏱️ ~1 hr💰 Fish soup ~650 THB

An old fishing village beneath the Snæfellsjökull glacier · have lunch at Fjöruhúsið, a bayside café famous for its fish soup, with views of the Valasnös cliffs

🧭 Navigate

⭐ Cliff walk: Hellnar → Arnarstapi

⏱️ Round trip ~2 hr (2.5 km each way)💰 Free

The peninsula's most beautiful lava-cliff walk, passing the Gatklettur rock arch + sea stacks + caves, with Arctic terns swirling overhead · too lazy for the long walk: drive to Arnarstapi and do a short loop around Gatklettur instead

🧭 Navigate

Búðakirkja — Black Church

⏱️ 20–30 min💰 Free

A black wooden church from 1847 standing alone in the Búðahraun lava field with a glacier backdrop, the last photo spot before leaving the peninsula → from here it's a long drive into Keflavík ~2 hr 45 min (can rest at Borgarnes)

🧭 Navigate
🛏️ Stay: Keflavík · Rokkheimur Studio (night 1/2) — self check-in via lockbox, arrive ~20:30–21:00

Explore near the airport → soak in the hot spring at the 20:00 slot (Best Rate)
🚗 Total ~1.5 hr📍 6 stops🛏️ Stay Keflavík

Miðlína Bridge Between Continents

⏱️ 20–30 min💰 Free

A footbridge across the rift between the North American and Eurasian plates — cross between continents in a few steps

🧭 Navigate

Gunnuhver

⏱️ 20–30 min💰 Free

Iceland's largest boiling mud pool + steam vents, with a boardwalk to view them up close (watch out for the hot steam)

🧭 Navigate

Reykjanesviti Lighthouse + Valahnúkamöl Cliffs

⏱️ 30–45 min💰 Free

Iceland's oldest lighthouse on a hill + lava-rock cliffs, sea stacks, lots of seabirds, and endless ocean views

🧭 Navigate

Brimketill — Optional

Optional⏱️ 15–20 min💰 Free

A natural lava-rock pool by the sea where waves crash in beautifully, with a safe viewing platform (don't go near the water)

🧭 Navigate

The Giantess in the Cave

⏱️ 20–30 min💰 Free (10:00–17:00)

A model cave by Keflavík harbor with a kindly giantess from a children's tale snoring in a rocking chair, plus giant-sized props; fun for photos and suitable for all ages

🧭 Navigate

Viking World — Optional

Optional⏱️ 45–60 min💰 ~390 THB (check website)

A museum with a full-size replica Viking ship, "Íslendingur," that actually sailed across the ocean + a Viking-age exhibition, with a café

⭐ Blue Lagoon — 20:00 slot (Best Rate)

⏱️ 2–2.5 hr💰 Comfort from ~3,120 THB

A milky-blue hot spring, soaking under the midnight sun · book the 20:00 · 31 Jul slot in advance (fills up fast) · out of the water by 22:30, closes 23:00 · ~25 min back to the accommodation · 🌋 check the volcano status at safetravel.is before going
